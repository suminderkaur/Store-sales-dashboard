# Store-sales-dashboard

Store Sales & Customer Insights Dashboard: 2024 Performance Analysis

# Project Overview
An interactive performance report tracking monthly revenue trends, geographic distribution, and customer brand preferences.

# Tech stack
*Power BI Desktop: Main data tool which is used to create the interactive report. 
*Data cleaning: Streamlined the dataset using excel and power query. 
*Data Transformation: Crafyed fact and dimension table based on primary and foreign key relationships. 
*DAX (Data Analysis Expressions): Used for creating calculated measures, dynamic visuals and conditional logic. 
*File Format: .pbix for development and .png for dashboard previews.

# Data Source
https://datadna.onyxdata.co.uk/

# Key Insights
The business generated $42.7M in total revenue over the year, moving a total volume of 54K units. Positive Growth Trait: The overall year-over-year momentum is highly stable, showing a healthy 9.88% growth rate.
The year launched strong with a January peak of $4.12M, followed by an excellent mid-year summer surge in July reaching $3.96M. Sales hit their absolute lowest valleys in April ($3.01M) and September ($2.98M).
Geographic Market Breakdown : India is undisputed primary market, contributing $20.6M—nearly half of the company’s total worldwide sales. Turkey represents a massive secondary revenue pillar at $16.0M.
The Online storefront is the most powerful channel by far, securing $27M of the overall portfolio. Samsung (24.25% share / $10M) and Apple (24.04% share / $10M) are locked in a dead heat for the top position, collectively owning nearly half the total market.
Revenue is heavily driven by adults aged 26–33 and 42–49. These two demographics represent the most profitable customer profiles.
